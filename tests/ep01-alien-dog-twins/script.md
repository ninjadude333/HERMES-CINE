**SCENE 1 — SUBURBAN STREET, LATE AFTERNOON**
Twin brothers walk their brown-and-white Border Collie puppy down a quiet tree-lined street, golden-hour light. One twin holds the leash loosely, laughing at something the other said. The puppy trots ahead, ears bouncing, tugging toward a patch of grass.

**SCENE 2 — SUBURBAN STREET, MOMENTS LATER**
A low hum rises. The twins look up. A sleek, rounded alien craft descends silently between the rooftops, throwing a cool blue glow across the street. The puppy freezes, ears back, tail low.

**SCENE 3 — ABDUCTION BEAT**
A soft beam of light sweeps down from the craft's underside and locks onto the puppy. The leash goes taut, then slips from the twin's hand as the puppy lifts gently off the ground, yelping — more startled than hurt. The craft pulls the puppy up and inside; the beam cuts off, and the ship rockets skyward and vanishes.

**SCENE 4 — STREET, IMMEDIATE AFTERMATH**
The twins stand frozen, staring at the empty sky, then at each other. Beat of shocked silence. One twin snaps out of it first, grabs the other's arm.

**SCENE 5 — RUN HOME**
The twins sprint down the street toward home, breathless, panicked, glancing back at the sky.

**SCENE 6 — HOME, GARAGE/WORKSHOP**
The twins burst in on their father, mid-project at a workbench cluttered with gadgets and tools — clearly the "tech-savvy" type. They talk over each other, pointing at the sky, trying to explain what just happened. Father's expression shifts from confusion to alarm to something like intrigued determination.

**END OF EPISODE 1 — CLIFFHANGER: Father grabs a device off the workbench and says, "Show me exactly where."**
